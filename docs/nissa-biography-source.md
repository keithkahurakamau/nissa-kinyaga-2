## Origins

Born and brought up as a bushbaby in northern Kenya, in the **Mukogodo Forest**. Walked
**7 km each way to primary school for eight years**. Learned as a herder boy in the
forest during weekends and school holidays, and on the walk to school learned all the
tracks and signs of wild animals: when a hyena had new pups, when an aardvark had dug a
new hole for termites, when buffalo had migrated north, the presence of African painted
wolves moving into the area, a bushbuck running for her life. Deep knowledge of the
general flora and fauna, and above all a deliberate knowledge of **herbal trees** and
the **symbiotic relationships among the bird species** of the forest and plains that
they call home.

Maasai cultural heritage is central. Specialised at college in **social anthropology** —
a wide range of human social life and tribes — which is fundamental to his tourist
circuit in the eastern and northern frontiers.

## Training

- **1997** — completed tertiary school level.
- **1998** — joined **Lewa Wildlife Conservancy**, ranger research department, as **head
  radio signalling officer**, the link between conservationists and the **Kenya Wildlife
  Service**, the state wildlife watchdog, for wildlife analysis supporting population
  growth.
- **1999** — joined **Kenya Utalii College** to specialise in safari guiding and
  administration: **wildlife biology, ornithology, social anthropology, dynamic cultural
  heritage, geology, astronomy, entomology**, among others. **Graduated with a
  distinction** in his final exams.

## Career

- **2002–2009** — **Lewa Safari Camp**, Lewa Wildlife Conservancy. Joined as a safari
  guide and rose to **camp assistant manager and head guide**. Resigned to expand his
  range.
- **2009–2011** — **Sirikoi Camp**, Lewa Wildlife Conservancy (Africa Exploration Ltd),
  two full years. Resigned for more exploration.
- **Three years freelancing** — Ol Donyo Lodge in the **Chyulu Hills**; **Angama Mara**
  in the Maasai Mara; **Sarara Camp** in the northern **Mathews Range**; **Saruni
  Kalama** community lodge; **Il Ngwesi Lodge**; **Tassia Lodge**.
  *(Source wrote "kalala community lodge (saruni camp)" — read as Saruni Kalama.)*
- **Six years at Borana Conservancy** — head of staff and head guide. Resigned in
  **2024**.
- **2024 to present** — back to freelance guiding, and running his own safari company,
  **Nissa Safaris**.

## What he does now

Specialises in tours and travel across **Kenya, East Africa, and southern and central
Africa**. His key roles, delivered "to my utmost good faith":

- Flight bookings
- Hotels and lodges
- Road transfers
- Private guiding

> "As I age, knowledge and wisdom accumulate for my guests' incredible comfort and epic,
> smooth safaris."
